Reading the prices

  • Coffee is in rupees per 50 kg bag, as a range of prices paid in Karnataka. Cherry prices depend on outturn, so a lot can sell above or below the range.
  • Futures are the world prices in New York (arabica) and London (robusta). Indian prices tend to follow them.
  • Pepper and cardamom are in rupees per kg. The cardamom figure is the average of the day’s e-auctions, weighted by quantity sold.
  • Prices at your gate will differ with quality, quantity and the buyer.

Endemic Greens copies these figures from the official reports linked on each card and does not change them. Sources publish on working days, so weekend and holiday dates are missing. If a source cannot be read, the card says so and keeps the last figures it read. The Coffee Board notes that it does not guarantee the accuracy of its report; neither do we.
