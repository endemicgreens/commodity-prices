Coffee, pepper and cardamom price data

Data files used by endemicgreens.com. Prices are copied twice a day from the Coffee Board of India and the Spices Board of India, with their dates and sources.

  • prices.json: latest figures, source, report date and status of each section.
  • history.json: one record per report date, for charts.

For current prices, read them on endemicgreens.com or at the official sources.
